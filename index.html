TOP UP HELP

Common questions from gamers

How fast will I get my top-up?+

Top-ups are delivered as soon as your payment receipt is verified. In most cases the diamonds are in your game account within minutes of confirming the payment.

Is it safe to top up from Digi Prime Nepal?+

Absolutely. Your game account and payment information are kept secure. We only ever need your Player ID — never your game password.

What do I need to provide for a Free Fire top-up?+

Just your correct Free Fire Player ID (UID) and the package you want. You can find your UID inside Free Fire on your profile screen.

Which payment methods can I use?+

eSewa, Khalti, and bank transfer/QR. You'll confirm your preferred method before sending the order on WhatsApp.

What if my top-up is delayed?+

Delays are rare. If anything looks off, message us on WhatsApp with your order details — our support team is online 24/7.
